WEBP to JPG Converter

Drop a WEBP image and it converts to a universally supported JPG automatically — free, fast and 100% private. Everything happens right here in your browser; your images are never uploaded.

How to convert WEBP to JPG

  1. Add your images. Drag WEBP files onto the box above, or tap “Choose files”.
  2. Let it convert. Each image converts automatically. Open Options to set quality, the background colour for transparent areas, or to resize.
  3. Download. Save each JPG, or grab them all at once as a ZIP.

Why convert WEBP to JPG?

WEBP is efficient and common on the web, but plenty of desktop programs, older phones and printing services still expect JPG. Converting makes your images open anywhere without extra software.

Is it really private?

Yes. Everything runs inside your own browser with JavaScript. No image is ever sent to a server.

My browser says it can’t read the WEBP. Why?

A small number of very old browsers cannot decode WEBP. If that happens you will see a friendly message — open the page in an up-to-date Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox and it will work.
