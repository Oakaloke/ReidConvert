PDF to Word Converter

Drop a PDF and get a fully editable Word document — free, fast and 100% private. Digital PDFs keep their real text; scanned pages are read automatically with built-in OCR. Everything happens right here in your browser and nothing is ever uploaded.

How to convert PDF to Word

  1. Add your PDFs. Drag PDF files onto the box above, or tap “Choose files” to pick them from your phone or computer.
  2. Let it convert. Each PDF converts automatically. Pages with real text are extracted exactly; scanned or image pages are read with OCR. Want to pick an OCR language or quality? Open Options.
  3. Download. Save each Word file with its Download button, or grab them all at once as a ZIP. Open the result in Microsoft Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice and edit away.

Smart text + OCR, automatically

Most PDF-to-Word tools either ruin a clean digital PDF by running OCR on everything, or fail completely on scans. ReidConvert checks each page: if it already contains genuine, selectable text, that exact text is used for the cleanest possible result. If a page is a scan or an image, it is rendered and read with optical character recognition. Mixed documents — some typed pages, some scanned — are handled page by page.

Is it really private?

Yes. Everything runs inside your own browser with JavaScript. Your PDF is never sent to a server — there is nothing to upload and nothing stored online. The only thing downloaded is the open-source OCR language model (once), so recognition can run locally on your device.

Does it work on scanned documents?

Yes. ReidConvert detects pages that have little or no real text and reads them with built-in OCR, so you get editable words even from a scan or a photographed page. Choose the recognition language and quality under Options.

Will the formatting be perfect?

The converter focuses on getting your text out cleanly and editably, with sensible paragraphs and a page break between pages. Complex multi-column layouts, tables and exact styling may be simplified — you get text you can work with rather than a pixel-perfect clone. OCR accuracy depends on the quality of the scan.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

Not yet — encrypted or password-protected PDFs can’t be read without the password, so the converter will let you know and skip the file. Remove the protection first, then convert.

Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes — it’s fully responsive and works in Safari on iOS, as well as Chrome, Edge and Firefox on desktop. Large scanned documents take longer on a phone because OCR is doing real work on your device.
