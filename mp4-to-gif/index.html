MP4 to GIF Converter

Turn a short MP4 clip into a shareable animated GIF — free, private and right here in your browser. Your video is never uploaded.

How to convert MP4 to GIF

  1. Add a short clip. Drag an MP4 onto the box, or tap “Choose files”. Shorter clips make smaller GIFs.
  2. Choose frame rate and width. Lower values make a smaller file.
  3. Convert & download. A high-quality colour palette is generated for crisp results.
Is it really private?

Yes. The GIF is made entirely in your browser. No video is ever sent to a server.

Why keep it short?

GIFs store every frame uncompressed-ish, so long clips become huge. A few seconds works best.
