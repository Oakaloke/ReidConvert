    How to convert HEIC to JPG

    1. Add your photos. Drag HEIC files onto the box above, or tap “Choose files” to pick them from your phone or computer.
    2. Pick a quality. 92% is a great balance of sharpness and file size. Raise it for maximum detail, lower it for smaller files.
    3. Convert. Press Convert. Each photo is processed locally and shown with a Download button.
    4. Save. Download photos one by one, or grab them all at once in a ZIP.

    Why convert HEIC to JPG?

    HEIC is Apple’s space-saving photo format, but many apps, websites and Windows programs still can’t open it. Converting to JPG makes your pictures viewable and shareable absolutely everywhere, while keeping excellent quality.

    Is it really private?

    Yes. Everything runs inside your own browser with JavaScript. No photo is ever sent to a server — there is nothing to upload and nothing stored online.

    How many files can I convert at once?

    Up to 10 per batch to keep things fast and responsive. When you’re done, press “Convert more files” and add the next batch — there’s no daily limit.

    My HEIC file contains several photos (a burst or Live Photo). What happens?

    Some HEIC files hold more than one image. ReidConvert exports each one as a separate JPG, named photo-1.jpg, photo-2.jpg, and so on.

    Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

    Yes — it’s fully responsive and works in Safari on iOS, as well as Chrome, Edge and Firefox on desktop.
