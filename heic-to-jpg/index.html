HEIC to JPG Converter

Drop an iPhone HEIC or HEIF photo and it converts to a universal JPG automatically — free, fast and 100% private. Your photos are converted right here in your browser and never uploaded.

How to convert HEIC to JPG

  1. Add your photos. Drag HEIC files onto the box above, or tap “Choose files” to pick them from your phone or computer.
  2. Let it convert. Each photo converts automatically at a high-quality preset — no settings to fiddle with. Prefer to tune things first? Turn off “Auto-convert” or open Options.
  3. Download. Save each JPG with its Download button, or grab them all at once as a ZIP.

Why convert HEIC to JPG?

HEIC is Apple’s space-saving photo format, but many apps, websites and Windows programs still can’t open it. Converting to JPG makes your pictures viewable and shareable absolutely everywhere, while keeping excellent quality.

Is it really private?

Yes. Everything runs inside your own browser with JavaScript. No photo is ever sent to a server — there is nothing to upload and nothing stored online.

Do I have to choose a quality setting?

No. ReidConvert uses a sensible high-quality preset automatically, so most people never touch a setting. If you want control, open Options to choose Smaller file, Balanced, High quality or a custom level, and to resize or rename files.

My HEIC file contains several photos (a burst or Live Photo). What happens?

Some HEIC files hold more than one image. ReidConvert exports each one as a separate JPG, named photo-1.jpg, photo-2.jpg, and so on.

Does converting remove my location data?

Yes. Conversion is done through the browser canvas, which strips embedded EXIF metadata — including GPS location and camera details — automatically. The photo’s correct orientation is kept.

Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes — it’s fully responsive and works in Safari on iOS, as well as Chrome, Edge and Firefox on desktop. On Apple devices your browser decodes HEIC natively; on other browsers an open-source decoder is used automatically.
