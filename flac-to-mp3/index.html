FLAC to MP3 Converter

Turn lossless FLAC files into compact, universally playable MP3s — free, private and right here in your browser. Your music is never uploaded.

How to convert FLAC to MP3

  1. Add your FLAC files. Drag them onto the box, or tap “Choose files”.
  2. Pick a bitrate. 192 kbps is a great balance; 320 kbps is highest quality.
  3. Convert & download. Tags like title and artist are carried over automatically.
Is it really private?

Yes. Conversion runs entirely in your browser. No audio is ever sent to a server.

Are my tags kept?

Yes — title, artist, album and other metadata are copied to the MP3.
