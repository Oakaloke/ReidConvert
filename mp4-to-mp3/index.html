MP4 to MP3 Converter

Extract the soundtrack from an MP4 video and save it as an MP3 — free, private and right here in your browser. Your video is never uploaded.

How to convert MP4 to MP3

  1. Add your video. Drag an MP4 onto the box, or tap “Choose files”.
  2. Pick a bitrate. 128, 192 or 320 kbps — higher sounds better and is a little larger.
  3. Convert & download. Tap Convert, then save your MP3.
Is it really private?

Yes. The audio is extracted entirely in your browser. No video is ever sent to a server.

Why does it say Beta?

Audio and video conversion uses a large engine that runs in your browser. It works well for typical clips, but very long or large files can be slow.
