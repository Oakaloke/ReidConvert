MOV to MP4 Converter

Convert Apple QuickTime MOV videos into universally compatible MP4 (H.264/AAC) — free, private and right here in your browser. Your video is never uploaded.

How to convert MOV to MP4

  1. Add your video. Drag a MOV onto the box, or tap “Choose files”.
  2. Pick a quality preset. Higher quality is larger; smaller is quicker to share.
  3. Convert & download. The MP4 is optimised for fast start-up playback.
Is it really private?

Yes. Conversion runs entirely in your browser. No video is ever sent to a server.

Why convert MOV to MP4?

MP4 plays on virtually every device, app and website, while MOV sometimes needs QuickTime.
