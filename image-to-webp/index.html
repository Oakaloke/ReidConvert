Image to WEBP Converter

Drop a PNG or JPG and it converts to a smaller, modern WEBP automatically — free, fast and 100% private. Everything happens right here in your browser; your images are never uploaded.

How to convert images to WEBP

  1. Add your images. Drag PNG or JPG files onto the box above, or tap “Choose files”.
  2. Let it convert. Each image converts automatically. Open Options to set quality or resize.
  3. Download. Save each WEBP, or grab them all at once as a ZIP.

Why convert to WEBP?

WEBP compresses far more efficiently than JPG or PNG while keeping excellent quality, so pages load faster and use less bandwidth. Transparency from PNG sources is preserved.

Is it really private?

Yes. Everything runs inside your own browser with JavaScript. No image is ever sent to a server.

Will every browser open WEBP?

All current browsers display WEBP. A few older desktop programs may not — in that case use our WEBP to JPG tool to go back.
