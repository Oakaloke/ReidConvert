JPG to PNG Converter

Drop a JPG photo and it converts to a lossless PNG automatically — free, fast and 100% private. Everything happens right here in your browser; your photos are never uploaded.

How to convert JPG to PNG

  1. Add your photos. Drag JPG files onto the box above, or tap “Choose files”.
  2. Let it convert. Each photo converts automatically to lossless PNG. Open Options to resize or rename.
  3. Download. Save each PNG, or grab them all at once as a ZIP.

Why convert JPG to PNG?

PNG is a lossless format that never adds compression artefacts, so it is the better choice when you intend to edit and re-save an image several times, or when you need crisp edges on logos, screenshots and diagrams.

Is it really private?

Yes. Everything runs inside your own browser with JavaScript. No photo is ever sent to a server.

Are PNG files bigger than JPGs?

For photographs, usually yes — PNG is lossless so it stores more data. For graphics with flat colours, PNG can actually be smaller.
