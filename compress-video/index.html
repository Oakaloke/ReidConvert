Compress Video

Make a big MP4 or MOV smaller so it fits email, chat or upload limits — free, private and right here in your browser. Your video is never uploaded.

How to compress a video

  1. Add your video. Drag an MP4 or MOV onto the box, or tap “Choose files”.
  2. Choose a level. “Smallest” saves the most space; “High quality” keeps more detail. Optionally downscale the resolution.
  3. Convert & download. The new size is shown on each row.
Is it really private?

Yes. Compression runs entirely in your browser. No video is ever sent to a server.

Can I hit an exact file size?

This Beta uses quality levels rather than an exact target size. Pick a smaller level or downscale to go lower; the result size shows on each row.
