    How to convert JPG to PDF

    1. Add images. Drag JPG or PNG files onto the box, or tap “Choose files”.
    2. Reorder. Use the up/down arrows to set page order; remove any you don’t want.
    3. Create & download. Pick a page size and orientation, then tap “Create PDF”.
    Is it really private?

    Yes. The PDF is assembled entirely in your browser. No image is ever sent to a server.

    What does “Fit to image” do?

    Each page is sized exactly to its image, so there are no borders. Choose A4 or US Letter if you want a standard paper size with margins.
