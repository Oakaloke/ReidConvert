One book. One yellow button.

Turn every chapter into one downloadable audio-only MP4, with natural AAC sound. No video, no upload, no word credits.

↓ Download whole book · MP4
Add a document to create a whole-book MP4.

Every chapter is included in its original order.

Best on a computer. Keep this tab open and prevent sleep while creating a long book. Audio is encoded a segment at a time into temporary local browser storage—not held as an entire uncompressed book in memory. Free disk space is required. Cancellation starts over; download the finished MP4 before clearing the document. Voice selection affects the export; player speed does not.

Unlimited use. Real-world limits.

No daily word allowance, paid voice API or subscription. Generation uses your processor, memory and battery. Long chapters become smaller listening sections so you don’t have to render an entire book at once.

Private text. Downloaded voices.

Only voice models and software are downloaded from jsDelivr and Hugging Face. Document text stays in this browser. The model is cached when your browser allows it; browser site-data settings can remove that cache.

Made for a little patience.

Keep this tab open while creating audio. Scanned PDFs need OCR elsewhere first. PDF reading order can vary; check the extracted text. Download finished listening sections for dependable phone listening. Background and locked-screen playback depend on your browser.
