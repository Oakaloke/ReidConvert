Merge PDF

Add several PDFs, drag to reorder, and combine them into one document — free, fast and 100% private. The merge happens right here in your browser; your files are never uploaded.

Drop PDF files here

or choose files from your device

Supported: .pdf · merged into one document

100% private — your PDFs are merged on this device and never uploaded.

    How to merge PDF files

    1. Add files. Drag your PDFs onto the box, or tap “Choose files”.
    2. Reorder. Use the arrows to set the order the documents should appear.
    3. Merge & download. Tap “Merge PDFs” and the combined file downloads automatically.
    Is it really private?

    Yes. The merge is performed entirely in your browser. No file is ever sent to a server.

    Are bookmarks and form fields kept?

    Pages, text and images are copied faithfully. Some advanced interactive features (certain form fields, bookmarks) may not carry across — the visible content always does.
