PNG to JPG Converter

Drop a PNG and it converts to a smaller, universally supported JPG automatically — free, fast and 100% private. Everything happens right here in your browser; your images are never uploaded.

How to convert PNG to JPG

  1. Add your images. Drag PNG files onto the box above, or tap “Choose files”.
  2. Let it convert. Each image converts automatically at a high-quality preset. Open Options to set the background colour for transparent areas, the JPG quality, or to resize.
  3. Download. Save each JPG, or grab them all at once as a ZIP.

Why convert PNG to JPG?

PNG is lossless and great for graphics, but photographic PNGs can be very large. JPG is far smaller and is accepted everywhere — ideal for email, web pages and sharing. Converting flattens any transparency onto a solid background.

Is it really private?

Yes. Everything runs inside your own browser with JavaScript. No image is ever sent to a server.

What colour replaces transparency?

White by default. Open Options and pick any colour with the “Background for transparent areas” swatch.

Does it remove metadata?

Yes. Because conversion is done through the browser canvas, embedded metadata is not carried over to the JPG.
