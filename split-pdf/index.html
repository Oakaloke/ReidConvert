Split PDF

Drop a PDF and pull out the pages you need — by range, every N pages, or one file per page. Free, fast and 100% private; the split happens right here in your browser.

Drop a PDF file here

or choose a file from your device

Supported: .pdf

100% private — your PDF is split on this device and never uploaded.

How to split a PDF

  1. Add your PDF. Drag it onto the box, or tap “Choose file”.
  2. Choose how to split. Enter page ranges, split every N pages, or make one file per page.
  3. Download. One file downloads directly; several download together as a ZIP.
Is it really private?

Yes. The split is performed entirely in your browser. No document is ever sent to a server.

What page numbers can I use?

Use any range within the document’s page count. Pages outside the range are ignored with a friendly message.
