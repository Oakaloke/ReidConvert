Compress PDF

Drop a bulky PDF and shrink it to a smaller file automatically — free, fast and 100% private. Best for scanned and image-heavy documents. Everything happens in your browser; your files are never uploaded.

This compressor re-renders each page as an optimised image, which gives big savings on scans and photos. Selectable text becomes part of the image — if you need the text to stay selectable, keep the original.

How to compress a PDF

  1. Add your PDF. Drag it onto the box above, or tap “Choose files”.
  2. Pick a level. Open Options and choose Balanced or High resolution — lower resolution means a smaller file.
  3. Download. Save the compressed PDF. The new size is shown next to the original.
Is it really private?

Yes. Compression runs entirely in your browser. No document is ever sent to a server.

Why did my text PDF not shrink?

Plain-text PDFs are already small and efficient, so re-imaging them can even make them larger. This tool shines on scans and photo-heavy PDFs. If the result is not smaller, keep your original.
