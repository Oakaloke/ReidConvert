About

Conversion that respects your privacy

ReidConvert is a growing collection of free file-conversion tools that run entirely inside your web browser. There are no accounts, no watermarks and no uploads. When you convert a file here, it is processed on your own device and never sent to a server.

Why we built it

Most online converters upload your files to their servers, where you have little visibility into how long they’re kept or who can see them. Modern browsers are powerful enough to do the conversion locally — so that’s exactly what ReidConvert does. Your photos and documents stay with you.

What’s here today

We’re starting with a fast, reliable HEIC to JPG converter for iPhone photos, with more image and document tools on the way. Every new tool follows the same principles: free, private, and simple.
