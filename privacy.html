Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026.

Your files stay on your device

ReidConvert performs all file conversions locally in your web browser. Your photos, documents, audio and video are never uploaded to our servers or any third party. When you close the tab, the converted files and originals are gone from the page.

What we collect

You can use every converter without an account. If you choose to create an optional account, we store only your email address and account timestamps, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase). We never see or store your password in plain text.

If you are signed in, we may keep a short conversion history — the file names, the converter used, output format and file size. We never store the contents of your files; conversions still happen entirely in your browser.

If and when we add basic, privacy-respecting analytics, we will describe them here.

Payments

If we offer an optional paid Membership, payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your card details; we keep only a Stripe customer reference and your subscription status so we know which limits apply to your account.

Third-party libraries

Converters load a small number of open-source libraries (for example, heic-to and JSZip) from a public CDN to run in your browser. These libraries process files locally and do not transmit them.
