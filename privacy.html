Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026. This is a placeholder policy and will be expanded.

Your files stay on your device

ReidConvert performs all file conversions locally in your web browser. Your photos and documents are never uploaded to our servers or any third party. When you close the tab, the converted files and originals are gone from the page.

What we collect

The site is static and does not require an account. We do not collect the contents of your files. If and when we add basic, privacy-respecting analytics, we will describe them here.

Third-party libraries

Converters load a small number of open-source libraries (for example, heic2any and JSZip) from a public CDN to run in your browser. These libraries process files locally and do not transmit them.
