Compress Image

Drop a JPG, PNG or WEBP and it is compressed to a smaller optimised JPG automatically — free, fast and 100% private. Everything happens right here in your browser; your images are never uploaded.

How to compress an image

  1. Add your images. Drag files onto the box above, or tap “Choose files”.
  2. Pick a quality. Open Options and choose Smaller file, Balanced or a custom level, and optionally cap the width to shrink large photos further.
  3. Download. Save each compressed image, or grab them all at once as a ZIP. Each row shows the new size next to the original.

Why compress images?

Smaller images load faster on the web, attach easily to email, and fit under upload limits. This tool re-encodes to optimised JPG — the best choice for photographs — with a quality level you control.

Is it really private?

Yes. Everything runs inside your own browser with JavaScript. No image is ever sent to a server.

I need to keep PNG transparency.

This compressor outputs JPG, which has no transparency (transparent areas become the chosen background colour). If you need to keep transparency, use our Image to WEBP tool instead, which compresses while preserving the alpha channel.
